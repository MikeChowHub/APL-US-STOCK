# APL US Stock Visual Decision Tree

This document decides which visual type should be used before any visual asset is created.

It sits between Rules and Templates:

```text
Rules
>
Decision Trees
>
Templates
>
Examples
```

The decision tree prevents different visual assets from drifting into the wrong role.

---

## 1. Core Decision Tree

```text
Need to generate a visual asset
│
├── Is it the main Blog hero / cover?
│   └── Use Blog Cover
│       Role: Narrative Cinematic Financial Cover
│
├── Is it for search / link preview / sharing thumbnail?
│   └── Use SEO Image
│       Role: Search / Share Thumbnail
│
├── Is it a research terminal, radar, ranking, or systematic data interface?
│   └── Use Dashboard
│       Role: Research UI
│
├── Is it for mobile-first social distribution?
│   └── Use Social Card
│       Role: Mobile-first Summary
│
├── Is it a separately distributed companion insight based on a structured observation?
│   └── Use Table Card / Information Card
│       Role: Companion Research Information Card
│
├── Is it explaining a numeric relationship?
│   └── Use Chart
│       Role: Visual data explanation
│
└── If none apply
    └── Ask for clarification before production
```

---

## 2. Decision Checklist

Before choosing a template, confirm:

- Visual Type;
- Purpose;
- Target platform;
- Required dimensions;
- Required content;
- Prohibited elements;
- relationship with article section.

---

## 3. Blog Cover Decision

Choose Blog Cover when the visual must create the main market story.

Use:

```text
Narrative Cinematic Financial Cover
```

Do not use:

- Dashboard;
- radar-only visual;
- table card;
- local shape illustration as final background;
- presentation-slide layout.

---

## 4. SEO Image Decision

Choose SEO Image when the visual must work as a search or share thumbnail.

It may share the Blog Cover metaphor, but it must be composed for thumbnail readability.

Do not simply crop the Blog Cover if the crop weakens the story.

---

## 5. Dashboard Decision

Choose Dashboard when the visual must present systematic Deep-Scan data.

Dashboard is for research interface, not market storytelling.

Dashboard must not be used as Blog Cover.

---

## 6. Social Card Decision

Choose Social Card when the visual must work on mobile.

Social Card should communicate one primary issue-specific message with only the supporting data needed for mobile comprehension. It must not reproduce the Dashboard's complete scan funnel, radar, Buyability and sector hierarchy as a smaller layout.

---

## 7. Blog Information Card Decision

Choose Table Card / Information Card when a publishing channel needs a separately distributed companion visual for one structured insight.

Use this for:

- Top Gainers summaries;
- Top Leaders summaries;
- sector structure;
- observation / implication comparisons;
- market signal cards.

Do not paste full CSV data into a card.

Do not embed or reference the card inside the formal text-only Blog manuscript. The card may support the same argument as a separately published same-date asset.

---

## 8. Chart Decision

Choose Chart only when the visual relationship is quantitative and the chart makes it easier to understand.

Do not use charts as decorative backgrounds.
